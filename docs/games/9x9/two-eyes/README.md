# Two eyes

Shown on the "Two eyes" slide, after "One eye" (`../one-eye/`). A black group in the centre of the board, surrounded by white:

- A 13-stone group with eyes at D5 and F5. A white stone in either eye has no liberties and captures nothing, so it's an illegal move. The group can't be captured.

The position is built as moves (3 white stones as setup, then 13 white and 13 black moves, Black last), so the slide opens on the finished position with White to play, and ← steps back through how it was built. `"demo": true` in `index.json` means it opens on the last move and has no score card.
