# One eye

Shown on the "One eye" slide, followed by "Two eyes" (`../two-eyes/`). A black group in the centre of the board, surrounded by white:

- An 8-stone ring around E5 whose only liberty is the eye. White plays there and captures all 8.

The position is built as moves (4 white stones as setup, then 8 white and 8 black moves, Black last), so the slide opens on the finished position with White to play, and ← steps back through how it was built. `"demo": true` in `index.json` means it opens on the last move and has no score card.
