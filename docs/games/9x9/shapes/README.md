# Shapes

Shown on the "Shapes" slide: one setup position (no moves) with four black shapes, one per quadrant, clockwise from top left.

| Quadrant | Shape | Stones (SGF) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top left | Ponnuki: four stones around one point, left after a capture | cb bc dc cd |
| Top right | Bamboo joint: two parallel pairs, one space apart | gb hb gd hd |
| Bottom right | Empty triangle: three stones in an L around an empty point | gg gh hh |
| Bottom left | Tortoise shell: six stones around two points, left after capturing two | cf df bg eg ch dh |

`PL[B]` makes Black move first when someone plays on the board.
