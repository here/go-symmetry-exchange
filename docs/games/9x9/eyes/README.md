# Two eyes

Shown on the "Two eyes" slide. Two black groups away from the edge, each surrounded by white:

- **Left, one eye** (eye at C5): an 8-stone ring whose only liberty is the eye. White plays there and captures all 8.
- **Right, two eyes** (eyes at G6 and G4): a 13-stone group. A white stone in either eye has no liberties and captures nothing, so it's an illegal move. The group can't be captured.

The position is built as moves (4 white stones as setup, then 21 white and 21 black moves, Black last), so the slide opens on the finished position with White to play, and ← steps back through how it was built. `"demo": true` in `index.json` means it opens on the last move and has no score card.
